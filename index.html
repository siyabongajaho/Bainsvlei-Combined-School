Our Story

Bainsvlei Combined School is a vibrant public, co-educational institution situated in the growing urban suburb of Spitskop, Bloemfontein. Built on government infrastructure within the Mangaung Metropolitan Municipality, the school functions as a critical educational hub for the local community. We operate as a proud Section 21 (Self-Reliant) Public School, which empowers our administration to manage financial resources efficiently to maximize learner growth.
